The name of the organization is Dr. Athula Chandrasiri’s Medical Centre. It is located in Panadura.
The medical centre is maintained by Dr. Athula Chandrasiri (MBBS – Sri Lanka), who works at Keethumathi Maternity Hospital, Panadura.
Dr. Chandrasiri is a highly trained medical officer with over 30 years of experience. He has also worked in many hospitals across the country. The medical centre has a state-of-the-art laboratory and a top-quality pharmacy.
The laboratory services are run by Sachithra Private Hospital and Dr. Chandrasiri. The laboratory conducts medical tests with internationally accepted standards, and the pharmacy dispenses medication based on prescriptions issued by the doctor.
The medical centre provides many modern facilities to its patients and serves more than a thousand patients per month.
